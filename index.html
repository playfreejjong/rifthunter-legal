RiftHunter (균열의헌터) Privacy Policy

Effective: September 12, 2026 · Personal mail update: October 8, 2026 · Developer: playfreejjong (individual developer) · Contact: playfreejjong@gmail.com

This policy explains how the mobile game RiftHunter (package com.freejjong.rifthunter, the "App") handles information. General gameplay does not require separate account registration. If you choose to connect personal mail, we verify your identity through Google Play Games and store account connection information on the developer's self-hosted personal mail server.

1. Information we process

  • Advertising: Only after you choose "Agree and start" on the privacy notice shown at first launch, the Unity LevelPlay SDK in the App collects the advertising ID, device information (model, OS version, language, country), IP address, and ad impression, click and reward records to show ads and grant in-game rewards. You can withdraw this consent at any time in Settings > Account/Data > Ad data transfer consent; only rewarded ads stop working.
  • Sign-in and cloud save: When you sign in to Google Play Games, Google provides your player ID, display name and avatar. Game progress is saved to your own Google account (the App's private area in Google Drive).
  • Personal mail: When you choose to connect, the App and Google verification provide your verified Play Games player ID, a server-issued mail recipient ID (UUID), your game ID (GUID) used for support lookup, nickname, app build and protocol version, installation ID (UUID), account connection and last-seen times, and mail content, rewards and claim processing records. These are stored in PostgreSQL on the developer's self-hosted server.
  • Personal mail server operation and security: HTTPS requests may generate server access logs containing your IP address, request time and accessed path.
  • In-app purchases: Product ID, order number, purchase token and time are provided through Google Play Billing. Payment card details are handled by Google and are never shared with the developer.
  • Support: Your email address and message when you contact us.
  • On-device data: Game progress and settings stored only on your device.

Connecting personal mail does not additionally collect your email address, full Google profile, full currency or item balances, or purchase history. Google authorization codes and Google access or refresh tokens are used temporarily for verification and are not stored on the server. Personal mail sessions are valid for 30 minutes; the server stores a hash rather than the original session token. Nicknames and game IDs help locate accounts for support; Google Play Games verifies account ownership.

2. Why we use it

To show ads and grant rewarded-ad rewards, measure ad performance and prevent fraud; to provide Google Play Games sign-in and cloud save; to post your nickname, profile image and best record to the Google Play Games leaderboard, where other players can see them (only when signed in; visibility follows your Google Play Games profile settings); to verify purchases and deliver items; and to answer support requests.

Personal mail information is used to verify recipients, send and process rewards for the designated account, prevent duplicate grants, recover interrupted processing for each installation, and handle related support and security issues.

3. Third parties and international transfer

Information is sent over encrypted connections, when the App runs, requests an ad, signs in, verifies a personal mail connection or processes a purchase, to:

  • Unity Technologies (LevelPlay) and ad networks mediated through LevelPlay (United States and other countries), for advertising: Unity privacy policy
  • Google LLC (United States), for Play Games sign-in, cloud save, personal mail identity verification using an authorization code and player ID verification request, and billing: Google privacy policy

Each provider keeps data according to its own policy.

4. Retention

Support emails are kept for 1 year after the request is resolved. On-device data is deleted when you uninstall the App. Cloud saves stay in your Google account until you delete them.

Personal mail account information is kept while you use the service. After an authenticated account deletion, we immediately remove or clear your Play Games link, support game ID, nickname, app build, protocol version and server sessions. Mail, claim and audit records linked to the recipient ID are retained for 90 days after deletion to prevent duplicate grants and resolve processing disputes. A scheduled cleanup then deletes the account, mail, claim, installation and request identifier records, and anonymizes audit records by removing player links and reasons. Database recovery backups rotate on a 90-day schedule; backups older than 90 days are removed by the next successful backup job. Previously accepted deletions must be reapplied before a restored service resumes. Expired sessions are deleted periodically. In addition to the current log files, the web proxy retains up to 4 weekly rotated access logs and 10 weekly rotated error logs for operation and security before removing older logs.

5. Your choices and rights

You can reset or delete your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads), or through the privacy icon shown in ads. You will still see non-personalized ads. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information by emailing us. We will respond within 10 days.

To delete your personal mail account in the App, use Settings > Account/Data > Delete account while online and signed in to the same Google Play Games account. You can also request deletion by email after ownership verification. Resetting local data or uninstalling alone does not delete server data. See Data deletion instructions for details.

6. Children

The App is not primarily directed at children under 14, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If we learn that we have, we will delete it promptly.

7. Security

We process only the minimum information needed, use encrypted (HTTPS) transmission, and restrict operator access to the personal mail server. Google authorization codes and Google access or refresh tokens are not stored; personal mail session tokens are stored as hashes.
